# Facebook and Instagram ads in Slovakia: what they cost and how to start

Facebook and Instagram both belong to Meta, and ads for both are set up in one place, Ads Manager. A single campaign can run in the Facebook feed, in Instagram stories and reels and in Messenger at the same time.

Below: what you pay for in Meta, what Facebook and Instagram ads cost in real figures, why the "boost post" button is not enough for a business, and how to launch a campaign that brings enquiries and calls, step by step. If you would rather hand it over, see [Google Ads and Meta ads management](/en/service/ads/): launch from 1,000€, management 25€/hr.

## What you pay for in Meta

In Google you pay per click. In Meta you almost always pay per **thousand impressions** (CPM). The price comes out of an auction and depends on three things:

- **the audience**: who you compete for with other advertisers; a narrow, sought-after audience costs more;
- **the campaign goal**: a campaign for purchases or enquiries costs more per thousand impressions than a reach campaign, because Meta looks for people who actually act;
- **ad quality**: Meta makes ads that people scroll past or hide more expensive.

So watch the cost of a result, not the cost of an impression. Cheap impressions for people who will never buy anything are the most expensive advertising of all.

**Budget.** Technically you can start with a couple of euros a day. In practice Meta needs about 50 results a week in one ad set to learn whom to show the ad to. For enquiries or calls that means **10-20€ a day for one campaign**. A smaller budget split across three campaigns gives neither results nor data.

**The work** is separate. A complete campaign launch costs about 1,000€ once with me, management 25€/hr, and you pay the budget straight to Meta from your own ad account.

**VAT.** Meta invoices from Ireland without VAT. If you are not a VAT payer, you must register under §7a before the first campaign and pay 23% of every invoice yourself: on 300€ a month that is 69€ extra. The details are in [Google and Facebook ads and Slovak VAT](/en/blog/slovak-vat-on-google-facebook-ads/).

## "Boost post" or Ads Manager

The button under a post is the quickest way to spend money. A boosted post aims at reach and reactions: likes, comments, profile visits. Likes are not customers.

An Ads Manager campaign can optimise for what keeps a business going: a submitted form, a call, a purchase. The condition is that Meta sees those events, which means the **Meta pixel** on the website and ideally the **Conversions API**, which sends events from the server and does not lose them to browser blocking.

RAM service shows the difference. The previous campaign was set to traffic: thousands of visits in three months and not a single recorded call. After the pixel and Conversions API went in and the budget moved to a calls campaign, 21 calls came in the first week.

## What to prepare before launching

1. **A business portfolio in Meta** (formerly Business Manager) and **an ad account in your company name**, not the agency's and not an employee's personal profile. When you part with a contractor, the account and its statistics stay with you.
2. **Billing details** with your company ID and VAT number if you have one.
3. **The Meta pixel and Conversions API** on the website, with events set up: enquiry, call, purchase.
4. **A cookie banner** that starts the pixel only after consent. Without it you break EU rules, and the ads lose part of the data as well. More in [cookie banners in 2026](/en/blog/cookie-banner-2026/).
5. **A landing page**: the specific service or product, not the home page.

## How to launch the first campaign, step by step

1. **Goal.** Leads (a form or a call) for services, Sales for an online shop. I do not use Traffic or Engagement for a first campaign; they optimise for exactly what you do not need.
2. **Audience.** Location and radius, for example Bratislava and 25 km around. There is no need to narrow interests too much: with a good pixel, Meta finds the audience itself.
3. **Language.** Slovakia also has Russian, Ukrainian and English-speaking customers. Each language gets its own ad set and its own copy, as with RAM service, whose ads run in four languages.
4. **Placements.** Automatic Advantage+ placements usually come out cheaper than picking only the Facebook feed by hand.
5. **Creatives.** Two to four variants: an image or video in 4:5 for the feed and 9:16 for stories and reels. The first three seconds decide whether a person stops.
6. **Budget and time.** Leave the campaign running for at least a week without changes. Any major edit sends it back into the learning phase.

## Why Facebook ads do not sell

- **The wrong campaign goal.** Traffic brings people who click, not people who buy.
- **No pixel or events.** Meta does not know who became a customer and has nothing to learn from.
- **A fragmented budget.** Five campaigns at 3€ a day, and none of them finishes learning.
- **The ad leads to the home page** or the profile instead of the page for the specific offer.
- **The same creative for months.** People stop noticing it and the cost per result climbs.

If ads are running and enquiries do not come, the full checking order is in [ads running, no enquiries](/en/blog/ads-running-no-leads/).

## Facebook and Instagram, or Google

Meta is strong where demand has to be created: the person is not searching yet, but the product catches their eye in the feed. Google is strong where demand already exists: the person searches for a service in words. For a small business with one budget I usually start with Google and add Meta when people need to be reached earlier. There are exceptions: Google does not accept ads from shops that repair other makers' devices, so for such a shop Meta is the main channel. More in [Google Ads or Meta](/en/blog/google-ads-or-meta/), and on paying per click in Google in [PPC advertising](/en/blog/ppc-advertising/).
