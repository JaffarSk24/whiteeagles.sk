# Google Ads for Small Business: A Step-by-Step Guide

For small and medium enterprises, Google Ads (Search Advertising) is one of the fastest ways to attract target customers. Unlike SEO, where results take months to appear, search ads can bring in your first orders on the very day of launch.

However, without the right setup, you can easily waste hundreds of euros with zero return. In this article, we will cover the essential steps to build a successful Google Ads campaign.

## 1. Choosing the Right Keywords

The most common mistake beginners make is using keywords that are too broad. 

- **Incorrect:** The keyword `websites` (too broad, high competition, many irrelevant clicks).
- **Correct:** Phrased or exact match keywords, such as `"e-commerce development price"` or `"laptop repair Bratislava"`.

Focus on longer search queries (long-tail keywords) that express a clear intent to buy or hire.

## 2. Setting Up Budget and Bidding (CPC)

Start with a modest daily budget (e.g., €5 to €10 per day) and use manual bidding with a maximum cost-per-click (CPC) limit. This prevents the Google algorithm from spending your entire daily budget on just a few overpriced clicks.

## 3. Filtering Irrelevant Traffic (Negative Keywords)

Negative keywords tell Google when **not** to show your ad. If you offer paid services, exclude words like:
- *free, jobs, career, diy, download, guide, course*.

For instance, if you build turnkey houses, you should exclude queries like "how to build a house by myself".

## 4. High-Quality Landing Page

Getting a click on your ad is only half the battle. If a user clicks through and lands on a slow, cluttered page, they will leave immediately. Your landing page must include:
- A clear value proposition in the main header.
- A concise list of benefits and pricing details.
- A simple contact form or direct links to messengers.

## Conclusion

Google Ads is an excellent tool for scaling your business, but it requires continuous monitoring and optimization.
