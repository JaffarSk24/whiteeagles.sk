# SEO Audit: How to Check and Improve Your Website

Does your website look great but fail to bring in clients from Google search? The problem might be hidden technical SEO issues that prevent search engines from indexing and ranking your content properly.

You don't need to spend hundreds of euros on expensive tools to find the core issues. A basic SEO audit can be done by yourself. Here is how you can do it.

## 1. Check Indexing in Google Search Console

Google Search Console is a free, essential tool for monitoring your website's search performance.

- Navigate to the **Pages** section.
- Check if Google reports any major issues, like "Discovered - currently not indexed" or "Not found (404)".
- If your key service pages are not indexed, they will not appear in search results at all.

## 2. Test Speed and Core Web Vitals

Website speed is an official Google ranking factor. Slow sites lose both search rankings and visitors.

Use the free **Google PageSpeed Insights** tool:
- Enter your URL.
- Focus on metrics like LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) and INP (Interaction to Next Paint).
- If your scores are in the red zone, your website requires code optimization and image compression.

## 3. Check Meta Tags (Title and Description)

The Title and Description tags form the snippet that users see in Google search results.

- Every page on your website must have a **unique** Title and Description.
- The Title should include the primary keyword for the page (keep it under 60 characters).
- The Description should be compelling and include a call to action (keep it under 150-160 characters).

## 4. Analyze Heading Structure (H1-H6)

Search engines read your website like a book. A clear heading hierarchy helps them understand what your content is about.

- There should be **only one H1 tag** per page (the main title).
- Subheadings should follow a logical order: H2, H3, etc.
- Do not use heading tags just to make text larger for design purposes (e.g., in the footer).

## Conclusion

A simple SEO audit helps you identify the main bottlenecks holding back your business online. Fixing these basic issues often leads to an immediate boost in search rankings.
