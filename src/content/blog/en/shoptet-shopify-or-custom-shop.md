# Shoptet, Shopify or a custom online shop: how to choose

Choosing a platform is a decision for years. A cheap start on the wrong platform is paid for two years later with a migration, and that costs more than starting right.

Below: how Shoptet, Shopify, WooCommerce and a custom shop differ for selling in Slovakia, where each platform hits its limit, and how to move without losing Google rankings. The three-year cost comparison of Shoptet and a custom shop is in [setting up an online shop in Slovakia](/en/blog/online-shop-in-slovakia/); I do not repeat it here.

## Four routes in brief

| | Shoptet | Shopify | WooCommerce | Custom |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | Czech hosted platform, monthly subscription | Canadian hosted platform, monthly subscription | WordPress add-on on your own hosting | a shop on its own code |
| **Slovak specifics** | invoices, shipping, gateways, Slovak language built in | much of it through paid apps | through plugins | built as needed |
| **Start** | fastest and cheapest | fast | medium | from 5,000€ |
| **Monthly** | plan, add-ons, payment fees | plan, apps, fees | hosting, plugin licences, maintenance | hosting |
| **Limit** | the platform's template and add-ons | apps and their prices | plugins and their compatibility | budget |
| **Who owns it** | the platform | the platform | you | you |

## Shoptet: the shortest route for an ordinary shop

Shoptet is one of the most widely used platforms in the Czech Republic and Slovakia. Its strength is that local needs are covered from the start: Slovak invoices, Packeta (Zásielkovňa) and other carriers, the payment gateways people here know, and product exports for Google Shopping and price comparison sites.

For a shop with an ordinary catalogue and standard logic it is a sensible start. The limit comes when you want something the platform lacks: your own wholesale prices, a configurator, unusual shipping. Then you start assembling paid add-ons or looking for workarounds. Plans and the three-year calculation are in [setting up an online shop in Slovakia](/en/blog/online-shop-in-slovakia/).

## Shopify: strong for selling worldwide, weaker on Slovak details

Shopify is the most widely used hosted shop platform in the world. It is strong in storefront design, in selling to several countries and currencies, and in its app ecosystem.

In Slovakia, however, many everyday needs are met through apps, each with its own monthly subscription: invoices meeting Slovak requirements, local carriers, some payment gateways. Also check whether Shopify's own payment gateway is available to your company. If not, Shopify charges an extra fee on every order when you use a third-party gateway. Shopify makes sense for a brand that sells abroad from the start and is built on its own look.

## WooCommerce: a shop on WordPress

WooCommerce is a WordPress add-on. It is free and runs on your hosting, so the data stay with you. You pay in other ways: updates to the core, the theme and dozens of plugins, security and speed. In the WooCommerce shops I repair, a common problem is plugins that stop getting along with each other after an update.

I do not build new shops on WordPress. If you already have WooCommerce, I will [fix it, speed it up](/en/service/bugfix/) or move it to another base. How to think about WordPress versus custom code in general is covered in [WordPress or custom](/en/blog/wordpress-or-custom/).

## A custom shop: when a platform is not enough

A shop on its own code is worth it when:

- **you have logic the platforms lack**: volume pricing for wholesale buyers, a product configurator, a link to your own warehouse or accounting system, shipping with your own rules;
- **you would assemble it from paid add-ons** whose monthly fees overtake the development cost within a few years;
- **you want the code and the data to be yours**, independent of a platform's price list.

An example is the [BODA BODA wholesale shop](/en/case/bodabo/), where the product page carries the parameters a buyer decides on. How wholesale differs from retail is in [wholesale online store](/en/blog/wholesale-online-store/).

With me a custom shop starts at 5,000€: payments, shipping, mandatory documents, Merchant Center for Google Shopping, purchase tracking and six months of support. After that you pay hosting and the payment gateway's fees, which every option has.

## How to choose: four questions

1. **Do you sell only in Slovakia and the Czech Republic, or worldwide?** The home market points to Shoptet; several countries to Shopify or a custom build.
2. **Do you have logic the platform lacks?** If yes, custom. If not, a hosted platform.
3. **How many products, and what change do you expect in two years?** Moving to higher plans and add-ons changes the calculation.
4. **Who will run the shop?** On a hosted platform you can do a lot yourself. On custom code you need someone who knows it, which is why six months of support are included in my shops.

## Moving from one platform to another

You can move from anything to anything. Products, categories and customers export, and usually orders too. The biggest risk is not technical but in search:

- **every old product and category address must redirect to its new one, one to one.** Redirecting everything to the home page looks to Google like the pages disappeared;
- **titles, descriptions and product structured data** are carried over, not rewritten from scratch;
- **the Google Shopping feed and purchase tracking** are set up before the switch, so the ads do not run blind;
- after the switch a new sitemap goes to Search Console, and for weeks you watch that the old addresses do not report errors.
