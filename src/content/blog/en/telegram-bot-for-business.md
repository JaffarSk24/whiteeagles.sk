# A Telegram bot for business: what it does and what it costs

Say "bot" and most people picture a chat window on a website trying to talk like a person. For a small business a different bot is more useful: a quiet helper in Telegram that does the routine for you. An enquiry from the website lands in a chat, you confirm it with one button, listings renew themselves, and you hear about a website outage before your customers do.

Below: what a Telegram bot actually does for a business, what development and running cost, and when you do not need one. If you already know what you need, see [Telegram bots in Python](/en/service/telegram/): from 500€, 25€/hr. Chatbots that answer customers on the website have their own article, [chatbots for business](/en/blog/chatbot-for-business/).

## What the bot does in practice

**Website enquiries straight into a chat.** A customer fills in the form on the website and the message arrives in Telegram, to you or the whole team. No logging into an admin, no checking e-mail. Whoever replies within ten minutes wins, and a bot makes that possible.

**Bookings confirmed with one button.** At [Krása štúdio OK](/en/case/studio-krasy/) a client picks a time on the website, the staff gets a message in Telegram, confirms it with one tap, and the slot is blocked in the calendar. The client gets a confirmation by e-mail. No spreadsheets and no extra phone calls. More about such a site in [a website for a beauty salon](/en/blog/beauty-salon-website/).

**Automatic listings.** Businesses that sell regularly through Bazoš, the Slovak classifieds site, spend hours renewing listings. The bot publishes and renews them on a schedule, at sensible intervals and within the portal's rules. For the timber shop [Severské drevo](/en/case/severske-drevo-google-ads/) a script posts products to Bazoš by itself.

**Alerts.** The website stopped responding, a form reports an error, an order above a set amount came in, stock is running low. The message reaches the chat at once, not with the morning e-mail.

**Messaging and data collection.** Messages to your own contact list of people who agreed to them, or regular collection of public data on competitors' prices.

## What a Telegram bot costs

| What | Price |
|---|---|
| A simple bot (website enquiries into a chat, alerts) | about 500€ |
| A bot with a calendar, a database or several systems | by scope, 25€/hr |
| Running on a server | a few euros a month |
| Changes after launch | 25€/hr, the first six months of support included |

**Messages are free.** The Telegram Bot API costs nothing; you pay only for the server the bot runs on. That is a practical difference from the WhatsApp Business Platform, where Meta charges for messages a business starts.

## Telegram when your customers do not use it

In Slovakia fewer people use Telegram than WhatsApp or Messenger; it is more common among Russian and Ukrainian-speaking customers. For most business bots that does not matter: the bot serves **you and your team**. The customer fills in a form or booking on the website as usual, does not need Telegram and never knows the bot exists.

If the bot has to talk to customers directly, first check that you can really reach them in Telegram. With a Russian and Ukrainian-speaking clientele you usually can.

## When you do not need a bot

- **A few enquiries a week**, and e-mail is enough. A bot speeds up the reply, but at low volume it does not pay off.
- **The process is not settled.** A bot automates what already works. If what happens to an enquiry changes every week, sort out the process first.
- **You want to replace a person in conversation with customers.** That is a website chatbot's job, and it has its own limits, which I cover in [chatbots for business](/en/blog/chatbot-for-business/).
