# Astana Ballet Theatre: advertising a tour in Vienna

The Astana Ballet State Theatre brought two performances to Vienna: the rock ballet Blind Forgiveness on 5 October 2026 and Two Carpets (Zwei Teppiche) on 7 and 8 October, both at Wiener Stadthalle. I did the whole campaign: a 5,000€ media plan, the campaigns, copy, banners and videos.

## The brief

Tickets were sold only on the Stadthalle website, and no pixel could be installed there. So people who opened the ticket page could not be retargeted, and the campaign had to find its audience by itself. There were two audiences: German-speaking Vienna and Russian-speaking viewers, for whom the name of the theatre is the main draw.

## What was done

- A media plan in three phases: early sales with a 20% discount, a warm-up, and a last call before the show. Half of the budget was kept for the final weeks, when most tickets are bought.
- Geography in two rings: Vienna and 10 km around it with top priority, the area up to 100 km separately. The Russian-language campaign also reached Brno and Bratislava.
- For the German audience the focus was the show and the genre, for the Russian-speaking audience the name of the theatre.
- Banners in every Google display size, each under 149 KB, and videos with voice-over in German, Russian and Slovak.

## Phase one in numbers

From 7 to 26 July 2026: 676,016 impressions, 21,610 clicks to the ticket pages, a 3.20% click-through rate and 0.027€ per click on average, for 580€. The rock ballet drew 12,933 clicks and Two Carpets 8,674, of which 4,433 came from the Russian-speaking audience. 83% of the budget was spent on phones. TikTok was not launched in phase one, and its budget went to Google.

After the phase, the offer changed from the early discount to "the premiere of the season", and Smart TV screens were excluded.
