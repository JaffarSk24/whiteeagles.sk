# Môj servis Kramáre: a Google Ads rebuild for a car service

Môj servis has been working in Kramáre since 2007: tyres, brakes, air conditioning, servicing, STK and EK inspections and a car wash. I audited their Google Ads and rebuilt the account.

## The brief

A Performance Max campaign sent tyre-service traffic to the price list: more than 1,200 paid sessions ended in a single conversion. Targeting was a 3 km radius, with no cost-per-click limits or negative keywords, and it had never been decided which action counts as a customer.

## What was done

- Campaigns split by service: car service, tyres, brakes, STK and EK, air conditioning, car wash.
- Every ad leads to the page of its own service instead of the price list.
- The whole of Bratislava instead of 3 km, cost-per-click caps of 0.85-0.95€, negative keywords. Air conditioning, for example, no longer shows for home split systems.
- The primary conversions are an online booking and a phone call, with cross-domain tracking into the booking system.
- A Looker Studio dashboard in Slovak for the owner: bookings and calls instead of raw sessions.

## The outcome

The budget goes to searches for services the garage actually sells, every ad lands on the right page, and the owner sees bookings and calls in one place. This case study is about the rebuild, without a before-and-after comparison of numbers.
