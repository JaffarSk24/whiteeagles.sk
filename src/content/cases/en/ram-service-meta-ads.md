# RAM service: Facebook and Instagram ads that bring phone calls

RAM service repairs phones, laptops, computers, consoles and Apple devices in the centre of Bratislava. I run their Facebook and Instagram ads in Slovak, Russian, Ukrainian and English, for Bratislava and 25 km around it.

## The brief

The earlier campaign was optimised for page views. Over three months it brought thousands of visits and not a single tracked call or enquiry. Google Ads is closed to the business: Google rejects ads for repairing other brands' devices under its third-party technical support policy, so the calls had to come from Meta.

## What was done

- The Meta pixel and the Conversions API were installed on the client's website, so calls and enquiries became visible to the ad system.
- The budget moved from the traffic objective to the calls objective.
- Each language has its own ad set.
- Five weak ads were rewritten, and the seasonal creatives are built around the brand's mascot.

## The result

In the first full week after the rebuild, in October 2026, the calls campaign brought 21 phone calls at 3.79€ each. The Slovak ad set alone brought 17 calls at 2.60€. Before the change, three months of traffic-optimised spend had produced no tracked calls.

It is one week so far, so I treat these numbers as a starting point, not a final result.
