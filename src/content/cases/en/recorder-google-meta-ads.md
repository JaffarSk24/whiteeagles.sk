# Recorder.sk: Google Ads and Meta for a refurbished electronics shop

Recorder.sk sells refurbished laptops, computers, phones and tablets from Bratislava across Slovakia and to neighbouring countries. The ads run in Slovak, Russian, Ukrainian and English.

## The brief

Refurbished electronics is a niche where clicks are cheap but buyers compare for a long time before ordering. The shop had no purchase event in Google Tag Manager, so neither Google nor Meta could see which clicks ended in an order and optimise for them.

## What was done

- A search campaign with eight ad groups, four languages times two lines (laptops and other devices), with a capped cost per click.
- Keywords that follow how people actually search: "použitý", "б/у", "used", "lacný". Hardly anyone types "repasovaný".
- Meta creatives in four languages.
- Purchase, begin checkout and payment info events (purchase, begin_checkout, add_payment_info) added in GTM, weak keywords paused.
- Meta catalogue campaigns for Slovak, Russian and Ukrainian speakers.

## The result

Over 90 days, from 30 June to 27 September 2026, the search campaign reached an 8.7% click-through rate at an average of 0.25€ per click: 1,765 clicks for 444€. The Meta ads reached a 3.87% click-through rate at 2.20€ per thousand impressions. Now that every checkout step is measured, the campaigns are moving from buying clicks to buying orders.

## If you have a similar task

If your shop sells something people take time to choose, the ads need to see the whole way to the order, or they will optimise for cheap clicks.
