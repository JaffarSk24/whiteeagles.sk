# Severské drevo: Google Ads for a timber shop, 324 purchases worth 134,684€

Severské drevo (SLOV-DV, spol. s r.o.) sells timber for house and garden: KVH and BSH construction beams, boards, glued panels, decking, thermowood, Siberian larch and tongue-and-groove cladding. Orders come through an online shop that delivers across Slovakia. Since spring 2025 I have been running its Google Ads and everything the ads need to work: analytics, the product feed and the website.

## The brief

The catalogue has hundreds of items, each with its own dimensions and a price per pack. People search for a specific material, and the ad should show them that product with its price, not the home page. The ads have to be measured by orders and their value, not by clicks.

## What was done

- **Research.** A keyword map of ten clusters, from beams and boards to sauna materials and "where to buy" searches, and an analysis of eight competitors including their ads.
- **Tracking down to the purchase.** GA4, Microsoft Clarity and Bing, funnel events and purchases with the order value. The budget was planned from the chain of impression, click, enquiry, purchase and average order, using earlier analytics and the company's internal reports.
- **Product feed and Shopping campaign.** Google Merchant Center with a feed that a Python script builds from the website by itself. The product with its photo and price appears right in Google results, at 0.04€ per click on average.
- **Search by material group.** Beams, boards, glued panels, decking, thermowood, larch and other groups each have their own ads and their own numbers. Groups that sold nothing or almost nothing in months (flooring boards, facade timber, exotic woods, sauna materials) are paused, and the budget went where the orders are.
- **Performance Max** for materials with a target return on ad spend.
- **The display campaign is stopped.** Its banners ran for a year in the Display Network and returned 0.97€ for every euro spent, so less than they cost.
- **Around the ads.** Metadata on every page, robots.txt, a sitemap and llms.txt, a cookie banner, updated Google Maps profiles, the Meta pixel and product catalogue, and a script that lists products on Bazoš by itself.

## The result

From 25 April 2025 to 12 October 2026, according to Google Ads: 324 purchases worth 134,684€ for 18,022€ of spend, so 7.47€ of orders for every euro of advertising. 161,316 clicks at 0.11€ on average.

The Shopping campaign works best: 26,529€ of orders for 1,871€ of spend, 14.18€ for every euro. In search, decking sells best (12.60€ per euro), then thermowood (9.68€) and glued panels (9.64€), while construction beams bring the most orders: 33,915€ for 4,724€ of spend.

The growth shows quarter by quarter: the best quarter of 2025 brought 53 purchases at about 52€ each, the second quarter of 2026 brought 112 purchases at about 35€ each.

An online shop with a large catalogue needs three things together: a product feed, search by product group and purchase tracking with the order value.
