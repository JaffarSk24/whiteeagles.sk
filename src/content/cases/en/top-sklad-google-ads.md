# TOP SKLAD: Google Ads measured by paid rentals

TOP SKLAD rents out storage rooms, boxes and containers in Bratislava and Nitra. Customers choose a unit and pay for it on the website, which I built as well ([website case study](/en/case/top-sklad/)). Since May 2026 I have been running their Google Ads: search in Slovak, English and Russian, brand and generic queries.

## The brief

Make the ads bring tenants rather than clicks, and make that visible in the numbers. An audit in August showed that directions, visits and calls (actions Google adds on its own) were counted as conversions next to rentals. The cost per customer by campaign could not be read, and automated bidding had nothing real to learn from. Part of the budget went to queries that bring clicks but no rentals.

## What was done

- **One conversion: the first rental of a new customer.** I check every rental from an ad against the website's order database to confirm it is a new customer. Google's report is not taken on trust.
- **Keywords without rentals are paused.** The August audit found four queries that spent 110.71€ in 30 days, 40% of the campaign, without a single rental. The money moved to groups that do bring rentals: Nitra was bringing a customer for about 12-13€.
- **Ad prices match the website.** The "from" prices in ads, sitelinks and price extensions are compared with the database every week, and a script corrects any difference.
- **Every change is checked.** A weekly report: what changed, what came of it, what stays. For example, a 0.70€ bid cap on the brand campaign cut the cost per click from 1.21€ to 0.55€ and brought almost twice the clicks in a shorter period.
- **Paid social is stopped.** Instagram and TikTok campaigns brought more than 8,000 sessions since June and not one rental, so that money no longer goes there.

## The result

The main search campaign holds a CTR of about 15% at about 1€ per click and gets 81-82% of the impressions it is eligible for. The budget does not limit it, demand does: search in this niche brings 1-2 new customers a week. That ceiling is visible in the numbers, and promising more on the same queries would not be honest.
