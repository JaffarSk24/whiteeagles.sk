# TOP SKLAD: a warehouse rental site with online booking of free space

Warehouse rental. A multi-purpose site with a booking system and management of free space.

## Why a brochure site fails here

A business card website is useless in this business for one reason: the client's real question is "do you have space of this size, and from when". A page saying "we offer modern warehouse facilities" cannot answer that.

So the client writes or calls anyway. A manager opens a spreadsheet, checks, replies — for every single enquiry. While enquiries are few, that is bearable. As they grow, the spreadsheet drifts from reality and promises start to conflict.

## What was built

A site where occupancy is not text but the state of a system.

- **Free space is visible online**, with no need to ask.
- **Booking runs through the site**, and a booked unit stops being available at once.
- **Occupancy is managed by the company**, and changes show on the site instead of living in a separate file.

That changes the shape of the conversation. The client arrives with a choice already made rather than a question.

## What it changed

An entire layer of repetitive correspondence disappeared. The manager handles enquiries that genuinely need a person, not ones that need a spreadsheet read aloud.

The second effect is trust. A company that shows its free space openly looks like a company in control of its operation. For a contract running months ahead, that weighs more than any slogan.

## Technically

Responsive layout, fast loading, forms with spam protection and verified email delivery. Analytics measures enquiries rather than views, so it is clear which pages and which unit types actually bring clients. Mandatory legal details and a cookie banner with Consent Mode v2.

Domain and hosting are registered to the client.

## If this sounds familiar

Any business selling a limited resource — units, boxes, slots, equipment — gains from showing availability on the site. It gains twice: less manual work and more trust.
